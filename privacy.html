1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Santos Hardwood Floors, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you submit a quote request through our Facebook (Meta) Instant Forms or contact us through this website.

2. Information we collect through Facebook lead forms

When you complete one of our Facebook lead forms, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Answers to short qualifying questions (for example, whether you are the homeowner and your project timeline)

We receive this information from Meta when you submit the form.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your quote or service request
  • Follow up with you by phone or email about your hardwood flooring project
  • Prepare and send estimates or scheduling details
  • Improve how we handle lead requests

We do not use your lead information for unrelated marketing lists outside these follow-up purposes.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your name, email, phone number, or other lead information to third parties.

5. Sharing

We may share information only when needed to operate our business or comply with law, for example with service providers who help us run email or phone communications, or when required by a legal obligation. Those parties are expected to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

6. Your choices — review or deletion

You may request to review the personal information we hold about you from a lead form, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, email santoswoodfloors@gmail.com and include your name and the phone number or email you used on the form so we can locate your record. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Data retention

We keep lead information only as long as needed to respond to your request, complete related follow-up, and meet basic business or legal record needs. You may ask us to delete your information sooner as described above.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect lead information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Our services are directed to homeowners and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our lead forms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of our forms or website after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
